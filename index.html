5 Expected Value

Traditional painted steel bollards typically require repainting every 1–3 years and are prone to rust and impact damage. Flexible posts eliminate repeated replacement of bent sign poles. Column Sentry units protect structural columns from forklift strikes that can cost thousands in repairs and downtime.

For many commercial sites, the payback period on bollard covers alone is measured in a single avoided repaint cycle — often less than 18–24 months.
