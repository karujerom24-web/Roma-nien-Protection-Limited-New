Our Commitment to Responsible Information Handling

This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how Romanien Protection Limited may handle information received through its website, enquiries, client communications and related business interactions.

1. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you interact with Romanien Protection Limited, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide when making an enquiry, requesting a quote, discussing security requirements or communicating with the company.

Examples may include:

  • Name and contact details
  • Company or organisation information
  • Phone or mobile number
  • Email address
  • Site or facility location information
  • Security requirements and service preferences
  • Information contained in enquiries or correspondence
  • Other information you choose to provide for a legitimate business purpose

2. How Information May Be Used

Information received by Romanien Protection Limited may be used for legitimate business and service purposes, including responding to enquiries and understanding client requirements.

Possible uses include:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for information
  • Preparing or discussing security proposals and quotations
  • Understanding site and protection requirements
  • Communicating about services and client arrangements
  • Supporting operational planning and client communication
  • Maintaining appropriate business records
  • Addressing service, security or administrative matters

3. Information Security

Romanien Protection Limited recognises that information connected with security services can be sensitive. Reasonable care should be taken to protect information against inappropriate access, use, disclosure, alteration or loss.

Access to information should be limited to people who require it for an appropriate business or operational purpose, subject to the applicable circumstances and arrangements.

Security-conscious handling includes:

  • Limiting unnecessary access to client information
  • Using information for appropriate business purposes
  • Avoiding unnecessary disclosure of sensitive site details
  • Maintaining professional confidentiality
  • Taking reasonable precautions when communicating information

4. Disclosure & Sharing of Information

Romanien Protection Limited does not intend to disclose confidential information unnecessarily. Information may be shared where it is reasonably required for a legitimate business, operational, contractual, security or legal purpose.

The appropriate handling of information will depend on the nature of the information, the relationship with the individual or organisation, applicable requirements and the circumstances in which the information was provided.

We aim to avoid:

  • Unnecessary publication of client information
  • Unnecessary disclosure of sensitive site details
  • Using confidential information for unrelated purposes
  • Publishing client names, logos or testimonials without appropriate permission

5. Client Confidentiality

Security engagements may involve sensitive information about facilities, assets, operating procedures, access arrangements and security risks. Such information should be treated with appropriate professional discretion.

Client information may include:

  • Facility and site information
  • Security requirements and risk information
  • Access and operating arrangements
  • Protection procedures and post orders
  • Incident and occurrence information
  • Technology and monitoring arrangements

Sensitive information should not be published on the website or in promotional materials merely because it is available internally. Client permission and appropriate confidentiality considerations should be addressed before publication.

6. Website Information

The website may contain forms, contact details and other features that allow visitors to communicate with Romanien Protection Limited.

Information submitted through a website form may be used to respond to the enquiry, provide requested information, discuss services or take another appropriate action connected with the request.

Third-party services

Website hosting, email, form processing, analytics or other technical services may involve third-party providers depending on how the website is configured. The specific providers and technologies used should be confirmed before making a definitive statement about third-party data processing.

7. Retention of Information

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, ongoing business communication, client relationships, operational records, contractual requirements or other legitimate purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances. Sensitive information should not be retained unnecessarily.

8. Privacy Enquiries

If you have a question about information you have provided to Romanien Protection Limited, you may contact the company and describe your enquiry as clearly as possible.

Privacy enquiries may relate to:

  • Information you have submitted through the website
  • How information is being used in connection with an enquiry
  • Client confidentiality concerns
  • Requests to clarify information-handling practices
  • Concerns about inappropriate disclosure or use

Requests will be considered in the context of the applicable circumstances and any relevant legal, contractual, operational or security requirements.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Romanien Protection Limited may update this Privacy Policy when its website, information-handling practices, services or business requirements change.

The version published on this page should be treated as the current website privacy statement unless a more specific written privacy or confidentiality arrangement applies to a particular client or engagement.

10. Contact Romanien Protection Limited

For privacy questions, client confidentiality concerns or general information-handling enquiries, please contact Romanien Protection Limited directly.
